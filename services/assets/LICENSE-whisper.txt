multilingual.tiktoken is Whisper's token vocabulary, copied unchanged from
the openai/whisper repository:
  https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/whisper/assets/multilingual.tiktoken
  last changed upstream in commit 839639a (2023-03-13)
  sha256 b34b360dbb493e781e479794586d661700670d65564001f23024971d1f2fa126
MeetScribe uses it to turn Groq's segment token ids back into text
(see services/transcribe.py).
